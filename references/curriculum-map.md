# Curriculum map — v2.0

The curriculum is progressive, but not every topic requires every stage.

| Level | Focus | Learner outcome |
|---|---|---|
| 0 | Orientation | Understand scope, purpose, prerequisites |
| 1 | Fundamentals | Explain the core concepts and vocabulary |
| 2 | Structure and normal function | Describe relevant anatomy and physiology |
| 3 | Mechanisms | Explain causal biological processes |
| 4 | Dysfunction | Compare normal and altered function |
| 5 | Measurement | Interpret what symptoms and tests can show |
| 6 | Interventions | Understand intervention categories, benefits, and risks |
| 7 | Integration | Connect the topic across body systems |
| 8 | Evidence literacy | Critically evaluate research and claims |
| 9 | Advanced analysis | Compare models and unresolved questions |
| 10 | Synthesis | Teach, apply, critique, and transfer knowledge |

## Recommended sequencing

1. Orient the learner and define the central question.
2. Establish the vocabulary and normal model.
3. Explain the mechanism before discussing dysfunction.
4. Introduce measurement only after the underlying physiology is clear.
5. Discuss interventions with evidence and safety context.
6. Integrate other systems where a meaningful mechanism exists.
7. Evaluate research and finish with synthesis and teach-back.
