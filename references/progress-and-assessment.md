# Progress and assessment guide

## Mastery dimensions

Score each from 0 to 4:
- Vocabulary and definitions
- Conceptual explanation
- Mechanistic reasoning
- Application to examples
- Evidence evaluation
- Integration and transfer

A suggested mastery threshold is an average of 3 or higher, with no unresolved critical misconception. Use judgment rather than treating the score as a clinical or psychometric measurement.

## Suggested review rhythm

Same session → 1 day → 3 days → 7 days → 14 days → 30 days.

Adjust based on difficulty, learner performance, and practical availability. The schedule is a study aid, not a guarantee of retention.
